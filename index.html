Good to know

How long does a clip take?

Allow about an hour per horse. Nervy or first-time horses take as long as they need.

How often should I clip?

Usually every four to six weeks from autumn through to late winter, depending on how fast your horse's coat grows back and how hard they're working.

Does my horse need a rug after clipping?

Yes. Clipping takes away your horse's winter coat, so they'll need rugging to keep warm, especially overnight and when turned out. Have rugs ready for when I finish.

What if my horse is wet or muddy on the day?

Clippers can't get through a wet or muddy coat, so please bring your horse in and keep them dry beforehand. If they're soaked when I arrive we may need to rebook.

What if I need to cancel?

No problem. Just let me know as soon as you can, ideally at least 24 hours before, so I can offer the slot to someone else.

Will you clip a horse that needs sedating?

If your vet advises sedation, please arrange it with your vet for the time of the clip. Your horse's safety, and mine, always come first.

How do I pay?

Cash or bank transfer on the day.

Do you charge for travel?

No, there's no call-out fee. Within about 40 minutes of Faversham you just pay for the clip. For yards further away, I need at least two horses to clip on the same visit.
